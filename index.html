01 / The idea

Stress-testing
visual reasoning.

Modern vision-language models can look remarkably capable—until a small change in the visual input or prompt reveals a brittle shortcut. JevAdvBench provides a structured way to measure these failures.

We study adversarial examples across visual question answering, object grounding, and multimodal reasoning, with reproducible evaluation protocols and human-readable failure cases.
